What a land contract means

With a land contract (agreement for deed), you take possession of the home and make monthly payments under a written agreement. You live in the home while you make your payments, and once the contract is completed as agreed, the deed and legal title transfer to the buyer. The full terms — including what happens if payments aren't made — are set out in the written agreement.

What you'll typically need

  • A down payment — agreed up front, based on the home.
  • Steady income — to support the monthly payment.
  • A commitment to long-term ownership.
