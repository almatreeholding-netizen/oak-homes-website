<design_decisions>
Read this before Task 2. These are settled — do not re-derive or re-litigate
them mid-task.

**D-A — Full-bleed needs no escape hatch.** `Layout.astro`'s `<main>` declares
only `min-height: 60vh` — no `max-width`, no padding — and `body` has `margin: 0`.
The current hero is narrow *only* because `.hero` itself carries
`max-width: 64ch; margin: 0 auto;`. Dropping those two declarations and moving
them onto a new inner wrapper makes the section span the viewport. No negative
margins, no `100vw`, no `calc()` tricks — those would risk horizontal scrollbars
and are not needed here. The two sections below keep their own container and are
not touched.

**D-B — The mockup's overlay FAILS WCAG AA. Use 0.62 / 0.72, not 0.55 / 0.66.**
`public/uploads/hero/home-hero.jpg` contains a blown-out sky region of literal
`rgb(255,255,255)` pixels. Measured over the real file (1500x844, 8x8
block-average sampling plus absolute brightest-pixel worst case):

| Overlay stops | Cream text worst 8x8 block | Absolute worst (pure-white px @ lighter stop) | AA 4.5:1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.55 / 0.66 (mockup as written) | 3.913:1 | 3.881:1 | FAIL |
| **0.62 / 0.72 (chosen)** | **4.914:1** | **4.878:1** | **PASS** |
| 0.65 / 0.75 | 5.440:1 | 5.402:1 | PASS |

The computed minimum lighter-stop alpha for cream text to clear 4.5:1 over a
pure-white pixel is `0.600`. `0.62 / 0.72` is the smallest round pair above that
floor that keeps the mockup's top-to-bottom darkening delta (+0.10) and its warm
near-black tone `rgb(26, 24, 20)` intact. It is visually a hair darker than the
mockup and materially safer. This deviation from the quoted mockup CSS is
deliberate and is the reason the contrast gate exists.

**D-C — Text color is `var(--color-cream)`, not `#fff` / `#f4f1ea`.** The
mockup's two hardcoded near-whites have no token. `--color-cream` (`#FFFDF7`) is
the project's own warm cream, visually equivalent, already declared in
`global.css`, and is what the "reuse tokens" constraint asks for. Contrast
figures in D-B are computed against `#FFFDF7`.

**D-D — Type sizes stay on the locked Phase 1 scale.** `02-UI-SPEC.md`
Typography locks exactly four sizes (16 body / 14 label / 24 heading / 36
display) and says 14px is the floor. The mockup's `48px` h1, `20px` lede, and
`12px` tag would each introduce a fifth size and break that contract. The hero
therefore inherits the global 36px h1 and 16px body, and the pill uses the 14px
Label size at weight 600. The banner gets its presence from the full-bleed
photo, the overlay, and 104px of vertical padding — not from a new type scale.

**D-E — Spacing snaps to the 4px grid.** The mockup's pill padding `6px 14px`
becomes `8px 16px` (visually equivalent, on-scale per UI-SPEC ## Spacing Scale).
`104px` vertical hero padding is the mockup's value, is a multiple of 4, and is
an intentional hero-only value — it does not introduce a new reusable token.

**D-F — The accent is a fill, never text.** `scripts/verify/checks.mjs`
`a11y-sweep` step 4 hard-fails any `.astro` file that sets a text color to the
accent value. The pill takes `background-color: var(--color-accent)` with
`color: var(--color-ink)` — measured at 11.93:1, comfortably AA.
</design_decisions>
