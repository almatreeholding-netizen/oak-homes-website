<objective>
Finish the phase by handing the assistant the thing that makes the panel usable without asking
anyone: a one-page printed cheat-sheet (ADMIN-06), and then proving it works by watching the
assistant publish a home unaided.

Per D-19 the sheet is a single Astro page at `/publishing-guide`, marked noindex, built from
Phase 1's existing brand tokens and printed to PDF once by the developer — not a Google Doc, not
a Canva handout. Keeping it in-repo is the point: an off-repo document drifts from the CMS the
moment a field label changes, and this sheet quotes the CMS's own validation copy back to the
assistant.

Two things are settled here before any copy is written. D-18 was deliberately deferred to
execution time: the owner does not yet know whether the assistant will use a separate GitHub
account added as a repo collaborator or share the Oak Homes account — and the sheet's sign-in
instruction cannot be written until that is answered. D-20 resolves the `[developer contact]`
placeholder to the owner at the existing public phone number, which the page reads from the
settings collection rather than hardcoding.

D-22 is a deliberate non-goal: the owner explicitly declined a Netlify build-failure notification
email. The "wait about 10 minutes after Publish, then call" instruction is the whole safety net
this phase ships, and no build-status tooling is added to compensate for it.

Purpose: success criterion 5 of the phase, and the only defense the assistant has when a publish
silently does not appear — the CMS surfaces no build status at all.
Output: `/publishing-guide`, an optional noindex prop on the shared layout, a sitemap exclusion,
a `publishing-guide` check, and a real unaided-publish test with the assistant.
</objective>
