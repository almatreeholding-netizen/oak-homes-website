<assumption_delta_decision>
The assumption-delta detector fired with a `optional` signal on this phase's scope: *"include the
fields Phase 3 reads (map coordinates, optional video URL, OpenGraph fields)"* — a required→optional
transition on the Property content model.

- **Primary noun:** `Property`, anchored on `slug`. The identity model does not move. A property is
  identified by its permanent URL slug (D-14 fixed `2734-brown-st` as safe to make permanent), and
  the new fields are attributes of that identity, not a competing one.
- **Decision:** `no-change`. The generalization here is additive optionality on a single entity, not
  a second kind of thing arriving where there was one. Nothing is being demoted, so there is no
  promote-versus-add-alongside tension to resolve.
- **Rationale:** `location`, `videoUrl`, and `ogImage` are optional *presentation* facts about a
  Property. A home with no video is still fully a home; a home with no pin is still fully a home.
  None of them is a candidate anchor.
- **The real risk this signal is adjacent to, and where it is handled:** the schema is about to
  become a two-file contract — `src/content.config.ts` here and `admin/config.yml` in Phase 2 — and
  those two files can silently disagree. That is D-06's `costly` reversibility rating, and it is
  recorded in SKELETON.md as a skeleton invariant. The suggested companion invariant test ("every
  field in the CMS config exists in the Astro schema and vice versa") **cannot be written this
  phase** because the CMS config does not exist yet; it belongs in Phase 2, in the same commit that
  first creates `admin/config.yml`. Flagged forward rather than skipped.
