If you've found this page while searching for a way to buy a home without going through a
traditional bank, you've probably come across the term "land contract" and wondered what it
actually means. Here's a plain-language answer.

## The short version

A land contract -- also called an agreement for deed -- is a way to buy a home directly from
the seller, with the terms written into a contract instead of a bank mortgage. You take
possession of the home and make monthly payments under that written agreement. You live in the
home while you make your payments, and once the contract is completed as agreed, the deed and
legal title transfer to you.

It's a straightforward way to buy on contract, with clear terms, a down payment, and predictable
monthly payments, all written plainly.

## How it's different from renting

With a land contract, your monthly payments are building toward ownership, not just covering the
cost of a place to stay for another month. The agreement spells out a down payment, a monthly
payment amount, and a length of time -- all agreed up front, in plain language, with no hidden
steps.

## What you'll typically need

- **A down payment** -- agreed up front, based on the home.
- **Steady income** -- to support the monthly payment.
- **A commitment to long-term ownership.**

## What happens if a payment is missed

This is one of the most important questions to ask before signing anything. The full terms --
including what happens if payments aren't made -- are set out in the written agreement for your
home. Read that agreement closely, ask questions before you sign, and reach out to whoever
you're buying from as soon as possible if you're ever having trouble making a payment.
